Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Rooftop Cat Run is published by Taalay Dev. This policy explains how information is handled when you play the game or contact us.

Your game progress is saved on your device. You do not need a game account. The current game does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not send your gameplay data to a developer-operated server.

1. Information saved on your device

The game stores your fish balance, unlocked and equipped cats, best distance, run statistics, completed missions, seasonal progress, visited areas, tutorial status, and settings such as language, music, sound, and haptics. It also stores purchase-entitlement information and transaction identifiers to avoid granting the same purchase twice, along with local timing information used to manage rating prompts.

This information is used to save progress, apply your preferences, deliver purchases, and operate game features. It stays in the app’s local storage; the game does not upload it to us.

2. Purchases and Apple services

If you make an in-app purchase, Apple processes the payment through the App Store. The game receives product information, purchase status, and transaction identifiers needed to deliver or restore eligible purchases. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple may separately process purchase information, diagnostics, ratings, or reviews under its own policies and your device settings.

See Apple’s Privacy Policy for information about Apple’s handling of data. The game does not implement its own cloud-save service. Your operating system may include app data in device backups according to your settings.

3. Advertising, analytics, and permissions

Advertising is disabled in the current configuration, and the game does not include a functioning third-party ad SDK. We do not use the game to track you across other companies’ apps or websites, sell your personal information, or send gameplay analytics to a third-party analytics service. The game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location.

4. If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include, such as a support question or screenshot. We use that information to respond and resolve the issue. Email providers process these communications to deliver and store messages. Please do not send payment-card information or other sensitive information.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to address the request, maintain necessary support records, or meet legal obligations. You can ask us to delete correspondence, subject to those obligations.

5. Sharing and security

We do not sell or rent support correspondence. We may disclose it when necessary to comply with a legal obligation or protect legal rights. We take reasonable steps to protect information we handle, but no device, storage system, or email service is completely secure.

6. Your choices and deletion

You can change game preferences in the game and manage purchase or backup settings through Apple. Removing the app and its data from your device removes its local save; offloading the app may retain that data. Backups and App Store purchase records may remain under your platform settings and Apple’s policies. Deleting local data can permanently remove progress and consumable fish balances. Eligible non-consumable purchases may be restored through the game’s restore-purchases function.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of personal information we hold, or to complain to your local privacy authority. Contact us to make a request. We cannot access or remotely delete your local save.

7. Children’s privacy

The game does not ask children to create an account or provide personal details. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through gameplay. Parents or guardians should supervise purchases and support communications. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can address it.

8. Policy changes

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The updated date will appear on this page. If an update introduces materially different collection or sharing, we will provide any notice or choices required before that processing begins.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Taalay Dev at a.u.taalay@gmail.com.
