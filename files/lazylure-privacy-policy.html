Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This policy explains how Lazy Lure (“the game”) handles information. “We” means the developer identified on the game's App Store listing. This policy covers the game; the App Store and any website hosting this page have their own privacy practices.

Your fishing progress stays on your device. Lazy Lure does not require an account and does not include developer-operated analytics, advertising, tracking, or a server that receives your gameplay data.

1. Information stored on your device

The game stores information locally so it can work and remember your choices:

  • Game progress: fish catches, collection records, in-game coins, equipment, aquarium decorations, requests, and the game calendar.
  • Preferences: language, audio levels, and other gameplay and comfort settings.
  • Game photos: images you create in photo mode, saved as PNG files in the game's local data folder. Photo mode renders the game; it does not use your device camera or read your photo library.
  • Review-prompt history: the date review bookkeeping began, launch count, request timestamps, and app versions already prompted. The game also checks current-session activity and progress locally to choose an appropriate time to request a review.

We do not receive these local files through the game. Lazy Lure does not provide its own cloud-save service. Device backups or operating-system services may copy app data according to your device and account settings.

2. Information we do not collect through the game

The current game does not request your name, email address, contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone. It does not include advertising identifiers, cross-app tracking, third-party advertising SDKs, or developer-operated analytics. We do not sell or share gameplay information for advertising.

3. Apple services and reviews

On supported Apple devices, the game may ask Apple's StoreKit service to display an optional App Store review prompt. Apple controls whether it appears and handles any rating or review you submit. The game receives no confirmation that you submitted a rating and does not store your rating or review text. Any public review may be visible through the App Store.

Apple may process downloads, transactions, device diagnostics, and other platform information under its own policies and your device settings. Apple may make certain reports available to developers; this is separate from the game's local save data. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Support correspondence

If you contact us at a.u.taalay@gmail.com, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, screenshots, or diagnostic files. We use it to respond, investigate the issue, and maintain necessary support records. Please avoid sending sensitive information. We retain correspondence only as needed for those purposes or applicable legal obligations. Your email or support provider may also process the correspondence.

5. Retention and deletion

Local game files remain until removed from the device. “Reset progress” in Settings resets gameplay progress, but does not remove preferences, saved game photos, or review-prompt history. There is no account to delete.

To remove all local app data, use your operating system's app-data removal options. On iOS and iPadOS, delete the app rather than offloading it. On macOS, deleting the application alone may leave its data folder behind; remove the game's data separately if needed. Copies in backups, exported images, and support correspondence must be handled separately. We cannot remotely access or delete files on your device.

6. Children

The game does not ask players to provide personal information, including their age. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the game. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us in a support message, contact us so we can address it and delete it where appropriate.

7. Your choices and rights

You can manage local data and backups through your device. You can choose whether to submit a review and manage Apple's in-app rating prompts through supported system settings. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of personal information you have provided to us, or to complain to a data protection authority. Contact us to make a request concerning support correspondence. These rights do not give us access to your device's local files.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. The effective date above identifies the current version. If we introduce materially different data practices, we will update this policy and provide any notice or choice required by applicable law before those practices apply.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email a.u.taalay@gmail.com. Include “Lazy Lure privacy” in your message.
