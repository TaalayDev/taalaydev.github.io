The Fun House Wants to Talk

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

This policy explains how TaalayDev handles information in The Fun House Wants to Talk (the game).

Information the game uses

The game runs without a TaalayDev account. It does not send gameplay progress, settings, or photo strips to TaalayDev. The game does not contain ads, advertising trackers, or analytics.

  • Progress and settings. The game saves your progress, endings, preferences, and unlock status locally on your device.
  • Photo booth. When you use the in-game photo booth, it renders images of the game scene and saves a PNG photo strip locally. It does not use your device camera or access your photo library.
  • Purchases. If you buy or restore the full game, your app store handles the transaction and provides purchase status to the game so it can unlock the paid content. TaalayDev does not receive your payment card details or store account password through the game.

For how the stores handle transaction data, see the Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Local game files remain on your device until you delete them or clear the game’s stored data. Uninstall behavior varies by platform. Your device may also include game files in backups you enable. Clearing local files does not delete your purchase history with the store.

Support and privacy requests

If you email TaalayDev, we receive your email address and the details you choose to include. We use that information to respond and keep it as needed to handle your request or meet legal obligations. You can ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you sent us by emailing a.u.taalay@gmail.com.

External links

Links in the game can open third-party websites. Those sites handle your information under their own privacy policies.
